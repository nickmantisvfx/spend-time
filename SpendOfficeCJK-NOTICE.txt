Spend Office CJK is a renamed, modified OFL font derived from Spend Office
(Jersey 10 with Cyrillic) and Fusion Pixel 12px Proportional zh_hans 2026.09.01.
Latin, Cyrillic and digits retain the original Spend Office contours and metrics.
CJK outlines are compacted and emboldened to fit the office lettering.
