Spend Office JP and KR: renamed modifications of Fusion Pixel 12px
Proportional ja and ko, release 2026.09.01, combined with Spend Office.
Contours are compacted and emboldened; original office Latin and digits remain.
Copyright The Fusion Pixel Font Project Authors and upstream authors.
See OFL-FusionPixel.txt and OFL-Jersey10.txt for complete licenses and notices.

Spend Office Arabic: renamed modification of Handjet (Rosetta Type).
Static weight 800, ELGR 2, ELSH 2. Arabic shaping tables are preserved.
Latin/Cyrillic letters and digits are replaced by scaled Spend Office outlines.
Copyright 2018 The Handjet Project Authors (https://github.com/rosettatype/Handjet).
See OFL-Handjet.txt and OFL-Jersey10.txt for complete licenses and notices.

All derived font software is distributed under SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Build scripts: tools/build-JP-font.py, build-KR-font.py, build-arabic-font.py.

Runtime optimization: JP and KR retain their original outlines for translated text and native syllabaries. Additional Han input uses the complete SpendOfficeCJK fallback. Original font licenses remain included.
